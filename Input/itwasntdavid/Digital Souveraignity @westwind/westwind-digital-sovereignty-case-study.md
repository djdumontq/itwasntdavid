# Westwind Hamburg: Real Field Missions Instead of SaaS Fees

For non-profits and grassroots initiatives, modern software quickly turns into an expensive trap: paying monthly per-user fees to Airtable, Notion, or Google Workspace for every single team member? With dozens of volunteers, software licensing eats up operational budgets—while sensitive community data ends up in closed American clouds.

**Westwind Hamburg** brings mobility to urban neighborhoods through cargo bikes and mobile repair workshops. Working together, we built an independent alternative: a lean, self-hosted platform running on a European server, tailored to their exact workflow—with zero monthly per-seat licensing penalties.

Here is what the system looks like in daily operation:

---

## 1. Mission Management (*NocoBase*)
The operational backbone: all mobile workshop runs, bike donations, wrenching days, and courses live in a unified relational database.
- **Transparent Dispatching**: Track live status (*in progress*, *completed*), duration, target groups (e.g. refugees), and assigned volunteers at a glance.
- **Instant Analytics**: Real-time dashboards aggregate volunteer hours (over 1,800 hours annually) and mission counts for board reports and municipal funding reviews.

## 2. Mission Scheduling (*Calendar*)
Field coordination made simple:
- **Unified Monthly Calendar**: Workshop days, donation pickups, and external events (such as foundation courses) are immediately visible across the entire team.
- **Role-Based Views**: Volunteers see exactly the shifts that matter to them—without exposing sensitive member records or private contact data.

## 3. Grant Management & Cost Tracking
Non-profit status requires audit-proof transparency:
- **Budget Allocation**: Missions and equipment purchases are mapped directly to specific grants and project budgets.
- **Accurate Expense Tracking**: Costs, receipts, and notes are logged centrally, making financial reporting to foundations and the City of Hamburg a matter of a few clicks.

## 4. Team Collaboration & Shared Brain (*Docmost*)
No more scattered PDFs or lost Google Docs:
- **Real-Time Wiki**: Collaborative editor for cargo bike repair guides, volunteer onboarding manuals, and technical decision records.
- **Institutional Memory**: Knowledge stays with the organization, fully searchable, protected from external AI crawlers.

## 5. Automated Daily Backups
True digital sovereignty means peace of mind:
- **Encrypted Nightly Snapshots**: Automated daily dumps of all databases and files sent straight to European S3 object storage (Scaleway Paris).
- **One-Hour Disaster Recovery**: Even in the event of hardware failure, the entire system can be redeployed from scratch in under 60 minutes.

---

## The Takeaway

No subscription penalties when onboarding new volunteers. 100% GDPR compliance on private infrastructure. **Digital sovereignty is not an abstract ideal—it is the most practical choice for organizations that value their independence.**
